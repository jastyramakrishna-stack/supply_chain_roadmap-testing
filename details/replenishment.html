Replenishment Planning

← Back to Roadmap

Replenishment planning determines when to order more stock and how much to order — triggering purchase orders automatically when inventory falls to a calculated reorder point. Poor replenishment is the root cause of both stockouts and overstock.

Core Concepts
🔁
Reorder Point (ROP)
Statistical trigger: lead time demand + safety stock = when to replenish.
📦
Min/Max Systems
Simple two-level policies setting minimum and maximum inventory bounds.
🤝
VMI
Vendor-Managed Inventory — supplier monitors stock and initiates replenishment.
📊
DRP
Distribution Requirements Planning — multi-echelon replenishment across network.
Replenishment flows — matching supply signals to inventory positions
P&G's VMI programme with Walmart reduced Walmart's inventory by 30% while improving P&G's forecast accuracy by 40%.

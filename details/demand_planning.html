Demand planning uses historical data, market intelligence, and commercial inputs to forecast future customer demand — the starting point for every supply, production, and inventory decision. A 10% improvement in forecast accuracy typically delivers 5–15% reduction in inventory.

Statistical Forecasting

Collaborative Planning
Cross-functional consensus between commercial, finance, and supply chain.
